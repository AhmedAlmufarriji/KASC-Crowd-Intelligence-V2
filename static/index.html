04 / SCOPE & INTEGRITY

About this build

◎

Independently implemented

KASC Crowd Intelligence V2 is an independently written prototype inspired by a collaborative UQU graduation project. The original code, datasets, and trained models are not copied here.

⌁

Research, not deployment

All 42 events, staffing patterns, queues, gate conditions and the stadium graph are generated for learning. No connection to ticketing, cameras, IoT, police, real attendees or official venue infrastructure.

⋈

What real deployment would need

Permissioned historical observations, an approved digital floor plan, secure ingestion, uncertainty estimates, physical capacity and evacuation constraints, accessibility validation, monitoring and human operational oversight.

See the original academic project

Preserving the authorship and history of the team project matters. This V2 is a separate demonstration.
